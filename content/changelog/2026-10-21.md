---
title: Skyfarm light, weather and mobs
description: Day and night, weather, light, torches and furnaces, then mobs and health in Skyfarm.
date: 2026-10-21
---

- **Skyfarm**: day and night, clouds, rain and thunderstorms, light levels, and torches, jack o'lanterns, glowstone and working furnaces.
- **Skyfarm mobs**: cows, pigs, sheep and chickens appear on grass in daylight; zombies, skeletons, creepers and spiders come out in the dark.
- **Skyfarm health**: ten hearts that slowly heal, swords, and falls, lava, drowning and the void now hurt.
- **Skyfarm witches**: rare potion-throwing witches that drop redstone, glowstone dust and more.
- **Skyfarm workstations**: all 13 villager workstations; villagers claim one and take up its trade, and the composter, smoker, blast furnace, barrel, cauldron and stonecutter work.
- **Skyfarm trading**: trade with villagers for emeralds; they level up, restock at their workstation, and give big discounts once cured.
- **Skyfarm zombie villagers**: cure them with a splash potion of weakness and a golden apple; harming heals the undead and poison can't touch them.
- **Skyfarm armour**: craft and wear armour; zombies and skeletons sometimes wear it, and iron and gold gear smelts into nuggets.
- **Skyfarm redstone**: dust, torches, repeaters, levers, buttons, observers, pistons, hoppers, droppers and dispensers now work like Minecraft's.
- **Skyfarm Store Depot**: a block that sends whatever goes in, by hand or by hopper, to your store to sell later.
- **Skyfarm land**: islands start as one 16×16×16 cube; buy more on any side, above and below too, from a 3D map in the shop.
- **Skyfarm away time**: your farms keep producing while you're away or the tab sleeps, at the rate they were running.
- **Skyfarm farms**: dropped items ride water streams into hoppers, pistons break melons and pumpkins, and farmer villagers harvest and replant wheat.
- **Skyfarm busy islands**: big redstone builds no longer slow the game down.
- **Skyfarm storage and market**: sell chests are gone; store depots send your harvest to storage, where you sell it to the system or to other players for site coins.
- **Skyfarm coins**: island coins are gone; the shop, land and generator upgrades cost site coins.
- **Skyfarm villages**: zombies hunt villagers, villagers run and call up iron golems, stray cats scare off creepers, and mobs hit back at whatever hit them.
- **Skyfarm flowers**: bone meal on grass now sprouts flowers too, short grass grows into tall grass, and tall flowers from the shop drop another of themselves for bone meal.
- **Skyfarm dyes**: dyes from flowers, bone meal, ink and cactus, mixed like Minecraft's, for dyed wool and colourful sheep.
- **Skyfarm sheep**: shear them with shears; they grow their wool back by eating grass.
- **Skyfarm squid**: squid swim in water three or more blocks deep and drop ink sacs.
